Iomedae, the goddess of righteous valor, justice, and honor, was once the herald of the deity Aroden before his mysterious death. After his demise, she has risen to take his place and subsumed much of the wavering Church of Aroden. 
She maintains a position of great importance to the Mendevian Crusades, being one of the most popular deities among the crusaders, and for providing the knowledge of how to build the Wardstones.
