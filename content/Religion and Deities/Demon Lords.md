Demon lords are powerful demons that rule a portion of the Abyss and command legions of lesser demons. Given that Abyss is infinite, as many other planes are, there is a nearly infinite number of demons who would aim to turn their power against the rest of the universe. Thankfully for the rest of existence, demon lords despise each other nearly as much as they desire to destroy the rest of existence, so alliances are infrequent and uneasy.

While it is usually difficult for demons to enter Golarion, the Worldwound has granted demons an unprecedented opportunity to attack the mortal realms. If the problem is not properly contained, demons may one day rule more than the Abyss.
