Deities in the world of Golarion are supremely powerful beings capable of granting power to mortals through divine magic. Each divine being is strongly associated with specific domains, such as wind, travel, or magic, and typically resides outside of Golarion.

While many crusaders of Mendev would consider the deities of Heaven to be the most worthy of veneration, deities have many varied sources; the deities of Axis represent order unmarred by good or evil, in contrast to the cruel archdevils of Hell, the celestials of Elysium revel in their freedom while the fey of the First World create chaos, and even the [[Demon Lords]] of the Abyss are capable of granting power to their worshippers, though could not match a true deity in raw power.
