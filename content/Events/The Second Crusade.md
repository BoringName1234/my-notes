The Second Crusade began when more organized demons broke through the blockade around the [[The Worldwound]]. The crusade continued for many years, until the fortress city of [[Drezen]] was lost. The crusade was considered to be lost after this, and [[Iomedae]] gifted Mendev with the knowledge of how to build [[The Wardstones]], magical artifacts that would prevent the demons from continuing the invasion into [[Golarion]].
