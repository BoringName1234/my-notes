The First Crusade was launched 5 years after the [[The Worldwound]] was opened, and it successfuly contained most of the demons who had crossed into [[Golarion]]. However, the disorganized demons that fought the crusaders were merely a bluff by the [[Deskari, The Lord of Locusts]]. The blockade surrounding the Worldwound was shortly broken when an army headed by the balor Kommoradesh charged through.
