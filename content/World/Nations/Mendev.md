Mendev is a nation located on the North East side of Avistan, composed of evergreen forests, snow-covered mountains, and icy steppes. While the modernized cities and forts of Mendev might trick you into believing that it has been a powerhouse for generations, the truth is that much of its development has been spurred on by the foreign interest in stopping the demonic hordes advance into the rest of Avistan, who send resources and crusaders into the nation through the city of Egede on the bank of the Lake of Mists and Veils.

Mendev itself was not truly unified until only a small time before the [[The Worldwound]] was opened, when Queen Galfrey re-organised the country into its current state.
