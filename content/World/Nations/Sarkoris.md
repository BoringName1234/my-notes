Before the appearance of the [[The Worldwound]], the land of Sarkoris was a savage land of warriors, witches, and shamans. It was once ruled by many tribes, who revered a host of different gods, spirits, and heroes. Despite Sarkoris consisting of many different peoples, there was shared culture and religion across many groups, such as shared monuments and sites of worship.

When the Worldwound was opened, Sarkoris was quickly overwhelmed by demons. Its people now lie scattered and unable to return to their ancestral homes, and the land itself has been twisted by the Abyss, turning previously verdant forests barren, and transforming wildlife into grotesque caricatures of plants and animals.
