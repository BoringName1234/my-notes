Kenabres, the heavily fortified crusader city, lies on the south eastern border between Mendev and Sarkoris. It contains one of the Wardstones responsible for preventing the demon's advance into the rest of the world. The worship of Iomedae is strong in this region, and Kenabres has gained notoriety for housing a faction of radical inquisitors, who seek to honor the [Lady of Justice](Iomedae) by hunting demons and traitors in pious witch hunts. Despite crusaders coming from many walks of life and countless regions, there is a deep inequality in the city, owing to the piousness of the inquisitors. In particular, tieflings are often singled out for their heritage, and believers of unpopular deities face greater scrutiny during investigations.
