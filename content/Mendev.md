The nation of **Mendev** (pronounced MEN-dev)⁠ in northeastern [Avistan](https://pathfinderwiki.com/wiki/Avistan "Avistan") is a land long defined by its conflict with the [Abyssal](https://pathfinderwiki.com/wiki/Abyss "Abyss") forces of the [Worldwound](https://pathfinderwiki.com/wiki/Worldwound "Worldwound").
