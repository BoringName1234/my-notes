The world of Golarion contains a myriad of magical and supernatural wonders, scattered across many continents and countless nations. But this world has attracted special attention from the deities of the Outer Sphere, due to its duty as a prison for the mad god Rovagug, who is prophesied to be the cause of the end of the universe.
