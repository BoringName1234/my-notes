100 years before today's Festival of Light, the [[The Worldwound]] was opened in the heart of [[Sarkoris]]. It allowed demons to enter the world in monstrous numbers, threating to destroy the world. [[The First Crusade]] was successful in containing the demons for a time, until a more organized force broke through the blockade. [[The Second Crusade]] failed, and resulted in the creation of [[The Wardstones]], preventing the demons from teleporting freely into the world, making it difficult for the demons to continue their conquest. [[The Third Crusade]] failed before it could fight any demons, falling to sabotage and infighting before any pitched battles could occur, and [[The Fourth Crusade]] produced similarly dismal results. 

Despite that, the festive mood in the city could make you forget all about the looming threat. Our adventurers find themselves at one of the squares in [[Kenabres|New Kenabres]] bordering a forested park, where the celebrations are in full swing. There are stalls of every kind around the edges of the square, and a jousting competition is going to be held soon. An aasimar cleric by the name of Elric stands at the doorway of the Sword and Board inn, and finds themselves drawn into a conversation with a boisterous dwarf who tells him about the jousting competition's prize pool. While he's not interested in participating himself, a well-armored artificer by the name of Lysander overhears, and after getting information from the dwarf (and having his entry fee paid for by the cleric) he joins the jousting competition.
Meanwhile, a tiefling named Jayne takes an opportunity to steal a candied apple from under a shopkeeper's nose, and stalks off before the theft is noticed. Taking interest in the dwarf's promise of money, she asks him for information about the tournament and accepts a bet with him; if she gets to the second round of the tournament, he'll give her 50 gold, otherwise, he gets 20 from her. After she 'encourages' him to sweeten the deal and lower her price to a single Nerosyan Mead, she fetches the drink and signs up to the tournament under the name Zonas. Unbeknownst to her, the final bet is being placed a stand near the inn, where a human druid named Drew is negotiating a bet with a croupier named Bertholdt. After Drew nervously places a 10 gold bet on a mysterious man named Grunt (?), the tournament is ready to begin.

The first round starts with a match between Lysander and a dark-skinned noble named Itamen. They meet in the middle, and Lysander guesses from the man's honorable nature that he should expect a straightforward attack, and he plans to counter it by weathering the blow. While he is unable to muster his horse as effectively as Itamen, his strategy works at first. However, Itamen managed to eek out a victory after knocking Lysander from his horse. Lysander moves to the edge of the road, saddened (and somewhat embarresed) by his loss. Shortly after, 'Zonas' faces off against Captain Zora, a confident orcish woman who captures the interest of the audience. After correctly guessing Zora's strategy and choosing to an evasive response, Jayne moves to the second round. In the final match of the round, the mysterious barbarian Grunt handily defeats his opponent.

Unfortunately for Jayne, her second match is with the enigmatic Grunt, and despite her efforts, she gains no insight on Grunt's deepest thoughts. While she manages to avoid being dismounted in the first bout, Grunt takes his inevitable victory in the second bout. While she collects her belongings and returns to the dwarf to collect her winnings, Grunt easily wins the tournament, fulfilling Drew's bet with Bertholdt. Both adventurers are 100 gold richer, and the day goes on at the busy square, with only a shouting match between Lysander and Lord Inquisitor Hulrun threating the festivities.

But the good mood is suddenly broken by the sound of thunder in the distance, and as a group dressed like guards suddenly rushes into a nearby building, demons emerge from the cobbled street and attack the citizens of Kenabres. The dretches from the group immediately rush at the people sitting outside the inn, and the adventurers are forced to defend themselves against the surprise attack. While Lysander, Drew, and Elric fight the bulk of the demons, Jayne duels a quasit that claims that everyone in the city will die. After a brutal fight, they interrogate the last demon, and find out that this group was commanded to attack the city by Minagho, a demon who tricked Staunton Vhane into allowing demons to enter the city of Drezen nearly 80 years ago.

A moment later, two of the guards run back into the square, and tell the group that they all have to leave or risk death. They see a pillar of red smoke emerge from the building, and from others around the city. Before they can discuss the situation further a shadow demon phases through the wall of the building, and the party is forced to flee through the alleyway to escape. While their initial attempts to slow the demon fail, they manage to hide along the main road and sneak past the demon.

With no other ideas, the party chooses to advance towards [[The Wardstones]], only stopping when they see a fight between the silver dragon Terendelev and the demon commander Kommoradesh. With no hope of being able to aid in that fight, one of the guards assures the party that Terendelev must feel confident in her chances, as she has not started using her deadly fire breath, which could secure victory at the cost of destroying much of the surrounding area. Instead, the party observes the square for activity, and spots cultists gathering bodies around a large ritual circle. Given the potential danger that could arrive if the ritual is completed, the group decides to intervene. The guard with a bow fires an arrow at a cultist, only for it to be revealed that the 'man' was actually a swarm of locusts taking a human form. Lysander rushes towards another cultists, only for them to dodge and plunge a dagger in their own heart. As all of the mortal cultists do the same, the ritual is hurriedly completed, and the locusts converge on the center. The party is only capable of watching as the swarm grows and grows, taking the form of a giant six legged and two armed locust; it is none other than the demon lord [[Deskari, The Lord of Locusts]], the butcher who leads the army of demons infesting Sarkoris. Terendelev realizes the danger of the situation, but is barely able to turn before Deskari opens a devastating wound across her heart. With his only equal felled, the demon lord mocks the party before attacking the Wardstone itself, with the aftereffect of the blow splitting the earth and causing the party to fall into the depths before Kenabres.

As they fall into the ground, the party is left to wonder how they will survive both the dark below and the burning city above their heads...
