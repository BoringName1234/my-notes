There were three tapestries in the room, covering the entirety of the far wall. The leftmost tapestry depicted an army being led out of gates by an angel. The second tapestry depicted a fight between the army and a group of demons, with strange symbols drawn around the demons. The third tapestry was far too rotten to be understood. 
When Elric stepped into the room, his training from the church allowed him to gain greater insight into the paintings; the first painting depicted crusaders of Sarenrae, a goddess of sunlight and redemption, being led out of Kenabres. This was an event that truly happened - around 80 years ago, at the start of the Second Crusade, an angel led an army into Sarkoris, never to be seen again. The second painting depicted an ambush where demons used curses to attack the crusaders. From the storytelling style, it was clear that this was a tragic telling of history, so the third tapestry should depict a sorrowful return to Kenabres. 

The group sat down to have a rest, funneling smoke from a campfire into the tunnel they had came from. 

When Elric touched the angel's sword, the lingering spirit of the angel granted him a vision. His saw the past through the angel's eyes, revealing the lost past:
	*When the demons returned to Sarkoris, I led the devoted of Kenabres towards the Worldwound.
	But when we encountered the demons they were far more organized than the chaotic mass we encountered in the First Crusade. We were led into an ambush, where demons rained curses and corruptive magicks on our host.
	I led those who survived back to Kenabres, but we were rejected for the corruption had tainted our bodies. I led my people deep underground, to shelter them from the fear of the people above.	
	Please save them. Please.
