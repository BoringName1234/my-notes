The Eagle's Watch is a group based in [[Kenabres]] that seeks to rout out saboteurs and cultists within the crusaders. Known only to the groups Commander, [[Irabeth Tirabade]], and discreet second-in-command [[Anevia Tirabade]], the group also watches over the crusaders and reports on the status of Kenabres to Queen Galfrey herself.
