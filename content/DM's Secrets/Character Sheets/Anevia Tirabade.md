[[Aenevia's Stats|Anevia Tirabade]] is a captain of the Eagle's Watch, and [[Irabeth Tirabade]]'s wife. She was born far away from Mendev in Nidal, a nation above unfriendly Cheliax, and grew up destitute with her mother. While wandering in search of a better life, she was captured by cultists worshipping Zon-Kuthon and nearly sacrificed, before her wife arrived to save her. For Anevia, it was love at first sight, so she followed Irabeth on her journey north to Mendev.
