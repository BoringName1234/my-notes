Defender's Heart is a tavern within [[Kenabres]]. A beautiful Tudor building made mostly of stone and surrounded by a high stone wall, it serves as the city's largest inn. It is located within the city's Gate District, and caters mostly to mercenary groups and crusaders.

The building has two floors for living, a large wine cellar, and an loft for lounging. The top floor has been converted into the Eagle's Watch's command center, with the map room being room being furthest from the stairs. Outside, a makeshift forge and repair station has been erected next to the stables. Wooden scaffolding has been added to the inside of the building to allow archers to shoot from the roof, and stairs allow defenders to guard the walls from atop the stable roof.
